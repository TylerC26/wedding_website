Our Love Story

It all began when high school classmates introduced Michelle and Tyler.

In 2022, when T returned from a business trip and had to spend days alone in hotel quarantine, M left a bag of snacks at the lobby front desk with a little memo: "Tough times don't last." Just a smiley face, no name. T knew it was her, and he still keeps it close to his heart.

When quarantine ended, T took charge again. He drove all the way from the west to the east to pick her up for work and bring her home, rain or shine. Weekends? He planned sweet escapes: cozy dinners, picnics, camping trips with friends, beaches, hikes…anything to light up her smile. Soon, they fell deeply in love. Growing together through every adventure, every late night talk, and every shared dream that made them stronger as one.

In 2024 at Niagara Falls Lake, with rainbows blessing the moment and the sea and sky as witnesses, T knelt. M said "Yes" through happy tears.

January 2026: A lifetime together💖
